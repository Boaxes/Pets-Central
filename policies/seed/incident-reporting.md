## 1. What to report

| Incident | Report to | Within |
|---|---|---|
| Customer or employee injury | Risk and Safety | 2 hours |
| Animal bite or scratch that breaks skin | Risk and Safety and Animal Welfare | 2 hours |
| Animal death in the store | Animal Welfare | 24 hours |
| Suspected theft or return fraud | Loss Prevention | 24 hours |
| Cash variance over $20 | District manager | Same day |

## 2. How to report

Use the incident form on the corporate portal. Include what happened, when, who was involved and any witnesses.
Photos help. Do not discuss the incident on social media or with the press; refer questions to Communications.

## 3. After an injury

Offer first aid, and call emergency services for anything serious. Never admit fault or offer compensation; Risk and
Safety handles that.
