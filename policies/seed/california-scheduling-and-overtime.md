## 1. Daily overtime

In California stores, hourly employees are paid:

| Hours worked | Rate |
|---|---|
| Over 8 in a day, or over 40 in a week | 1.5 times |
| Over 12 in a day | 2 times |
| The seventh consecutive day worked in a week: first 8 hours | 1.5 times |
| The seventh consecutive day: over 8 hours | 2 times |

## 2. Meal and rest breaks

1. A 30-minute unpaid meal break starts before the end of the **fifth hour** of work.
2. A second meal break is required for shifts over **10 hours**.
3. A missed, late or short meal break is paid as one extra hour at the regular rate. Record it on the timesheet.

## 3. Final pay

An employee whose employment ends is paid all wages owed **on their last day**. Request a final check from People
Operations at least two business days before a planned last day.
