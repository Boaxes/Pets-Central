## 1. Posting schedules

Post schedules at least **7 days** before the week starts. Changes after posting need the employee's agreement.
Stores in some states must post earlier; see the state policies.

## 2. Breaks

- Shifts over **5 hours** include an unpaid 30-minute meal break.
- Shifts over **4 hours** include a paid 10-minute rest break.

## 3. Overtime

Hourly employees are paid **1.5 times** their rate for hours over **40 in a week**. Overtime must be approved by the
store manager in advance, except in an emergency involving an animal's welfare.

## 4. Coverage

Every open hour has at least two employees on the floor, one of them a manager, assistant manager or key holder.
Every store with live animals has a caretaker scheduled every day it is open.
