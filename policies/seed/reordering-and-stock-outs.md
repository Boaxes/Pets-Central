## 1. Reorder points

Each product a store carries has a **reorder point**. When the quantity on hand falls to or below it, the product
needs reordering. The store system shows these under **Products → Needs reordering**.

Managers may raise or lower a reorder point for local demand (for example, more cat litter before a holiday). Do not
set one below **1**.

## 2. Ordering

Orders go to the distribution center by **2:00 p.m. local time** on Mondays and Thursdays and arrive in two business
days.

## 3. Stock-outs

1. A product at **zero** is out of stock. Check the back room and correct the count if any are found.
2. For the store's top 50 products by units sold, an out-of-stock must be reported to Supply Chain within **24 hours**.
3. Offer customers a comparable product, or an order for store pickup.
