## 1. Oregon: advance notice

Oregon stores post schedules at least **14 days** before the week starts. Changing a posted schedule at the
store's request means paying the employee a premium:

| Change | Premium |
|---|---|
| Hours added, or a shift moved | 1 extra hour at the regular rate |
| Hours cut or a shift cancelled | Half the hours not worked, at the regular rate |

Changes the employee asks for in writing carry no premium.

## 2. Washington: rest breaks

Washington employees receive a paid **10-minute rest break for every 4 hours** worked, taken no later than the end of
the third hour of each 4-hour period. Breaks cannot be combined or skipped to leave early.

## 3. Washington: sick leave

Washington employees earn **1 hour of paid sick leave for every 40 hours** worked, starting on their first day.
