## 1. Who hires whom

| Role | Hired by |
|---|---|
| Sales associate, animal care specialist, groomer | Store manager |
| Assistant manager | Store manager, with district manager approval |
| Store manager | District manager (corporate) |

Record a new hire in the store system (**Employees → Hire Employee**) before their first shift.

## 2. Starting pay

Starting hourly rates are set by role and state. Managers may start someone up to **10% above** the base rate for
experience. Higher offers need district manager approval.

## 3. Separations

1. Record the last day in the store system (**Employees → Terminate Employee**). Employees are never deleted: their
   sales and animal care history is kept.
2. Collect keys and badges on the last day.
3. Reassign the employee's animals to another caretaker before the last day (see Live Animal Care and Holding).
4. Final pay timing depends on the state; see the state policy if there is one.
