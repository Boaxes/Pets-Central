## 1. Count schedule

- **Cycle counts:** one department each week, so every department is counted at least once a month.
- **Full counts:** every quarter, in the last week of March, June, September and December.
- **Spot counts:** any product whose system count looks wrong to staff.

## 2. Correcting the system

After a count, correct the quantity on hand in the store system (**Products → Update Stock**). Enter what was
counted, not the difference.

## 3. Approvals

| Value of the adjustment (at cost) | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Up to $250 | Store manager |
| $250.01 to $2,000 | District manager |
| Over $2,000 | District manager and Loss Prevention |

## 4. Shrink

Shrink is stock lost to damage, expiry, theft or error. A store's target is under **1.5% of sales** each quarter.
Write off damaged and expired products the day they are found, and keep opened returns out of stock (see Returns and
Refunds).
