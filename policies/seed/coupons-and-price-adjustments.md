## 1. Loyalty coupons

Pets Central Rewards emails coupon codes to members, such as `DOG15-260622` (15% off dog products from June 22, 2026).

1. **One coupon per sale.** Coupons cannot be combined with each other.
2. The discount applies only to products in the coupon's department.
3. A code is valid only while its campaign runs. Expired codes are not accepted, even by managers.
4. Anyone with the code may use it; the customer does not have to be a member.
5. Type the code exactly as printed. Codes are not case-sensitive.

## 2. Price matching

We match the advertised price of a local competitor's identical product (same brand, size and formula), in stock at
that competitor. Price matching does not apply to online marketplaces, clearance or live animals.

## 3. Manager markdowns

| Situation | Manager may mark down | Needs approval above |
|---|---|---|
| Damaged packaging, product intact | Up to 25% | 25% |
| Discontinued product, last units | Up to 40% | 40% |
| Short-dated food (within 30 days of expiry) | Up to 50% | 50% |
| Live animal in store over 60 days | Up to 20% | 20%, from Animal Welfare |

Markdowns are recorded as a discount on the sale line. Never change the list price in the system; prices are set by
corporate.

## 4. Employee purchases

Employees receive 20% off products, not live animals or sale items. An employee may never ring up their own sale.
