## 1. Opening and closing

1. Every till starts the day with a **$150** float, counted by two people.
2. At close, count the till, remove the float, and record the count.
3. A manager or assistant manager signs off every close.

## 2. Safe drops

Drop cash to the safe whenever a till holds more than **$500** beyond its float. Two people are present for every
drop.

## 3. Variances

| Variance at close | Action |
|---|---|
| Up to $5 | Note it on the count sheet |
| $5.01 to $20 | Recount; manager investigates and notes the cause |
| Over $20, or three variances over $5 in 30 days | Report to the district manager the same day |

## 4. What is never allowed

- Employees ringing up their own purchases or those of family members.
- Lending from a till, or making change for personal use.
- Leaving a till open or unattended.
