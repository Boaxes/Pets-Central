## 1. Earning points

Members earn **1 point for every whole dollar** spent after discounts. To earn, the member gives their phone number or
email at the register. Points can't be added after the sale, except by Marketing.

Pets Central Rewards is run by our loyalty partner, not the store system. Staff can't look up or change a member's
points in the store system.

## 2. Redeeming

**500 points = a $5 reward**, issued automatically to the member's account. Rewards can't be exchanged for cash.

## 3. Tiers

| Tier | Lifetime points | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 0 to 1,499 | Points and coupons |
| Silver | 1,500 to 5,999 | Plus a birthday reward |
| Gold | 6,000 and up | Plus free nail trims at stores with grooming |

## 4. Campaign bonuses

Using an emailed coupon earns a **100-point** campaign bonus on top of normal points. See Coupons and Price
Adjustments for the coupon rules.

## 5. What staff must not do

- Promise points, rewards or tier changes that the program doesn't offer.
- Sign customers up without their agreement, or enter their contact details for them to earn points.
