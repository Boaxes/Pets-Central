## 1. What we record

The store system holds a customer's name, email, phone, postal code and whether they agreed to marketing emails, plus
their purchases. Record only what the customer gives us. Never record payment card numbers or ID numbers.

## 2. Changing details

Staff may update a customer's email, phone, postal code and marketing preference when the customer asks
(**Customers → Update Customer**). Confirm the change with the customer.

## 3. Requests to delete or see data

- A customer **with no purchases** may be deleted by a store manager (**Customers → Delete Customer**).
- A customer with purchases can't be deleted in the store, because their purchase history is kept for accounting.
  Send the request to Legal through the corporate portal, which handles it within **30 days**.
- Requests to see what we hold also go to Legal.

## 4. Sharing

Never share customer details with anyone outside Pets Central, or between customers. Managers see only their own
store's customers.
