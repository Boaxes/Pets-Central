## 1. Purpose

Customers should be able to return products easily, and every store should handle returns the same way. This policy
covers returns of products. Live animals follow the **Live Animal Health Guarantee**.

## 2. Return window

| Product | With a receipt | Without a receipt |
|---|---|---|
| Unopened products | 60 days | Store credit at the lowest price in the last 60 days |
| Opened food, treats and litter | 30 days, if the pet refused it | Not accepted |
| Opened toys, beds, equipment | 30 days, if defective | Not accepted |
| Aquariums, habitats and cages | 30 days, unused or defective | Not accepted |

A receipt is the printed or emailed receipt, or a sale found in the store system by receipt number or customer name.
A return can be made at **any** Pets Central store, not only the one that made the sale.

## 3. How refunds are paid

1. The refund is what the customer **paid**, after any coupon or discount on that line. Never refund the list price.
2. Refund to the original payment method. Cash sales over $100 are refunded by check from corporate within 10 days.
3. Partial returns refund the share of the line returned. Returning 1 of 4 bags refunds a quarter of what the line cost.
4. Record every return in the store system the same day, with a reason.

## 4. Restocking

Put returned items back on the shelf only when they are unopened and undamaged; mark **Restock** on the return. Opened
food and anything damaged is written off as shrink. Returned animals are never restocked (see the Live Animal Health
Guarantee).

## 5. Manager approvals

> A store manager may approve a return up to **15 days past** the window (so 75 or 45 days) with a receipt, once per
> customer per year. Note the reason on the return.

- Refunds over **$500** on one receipt need district manager approval before they are paid.
- Returns that look fraudulent (for example, many no-receipt returns from the same person) should be refused politely
  and reported under the **Incident Reporting** policy.

## 6. Cancelling a sale

A sale may be **cancelled** instead of returned only on the day it was made and only if nothing has been returned from
it. Cancelling puts its stock back and returns any animal to sale. Sales are never deleted.
