## 1. Every animal has a caretaker

Every animal in the store has at least one assigned caretaker, recorded in the store system (**Animal
Caretakers**). Caretakers are animal care specialists, assistant managers or the store manager.

## 2. Daily care

Twice a day, a caretaker checks each animal's food, water, temperature and behavior and records any concern. Any
animal that seems unwell is moved to the back room and seen by the contracted vet within 24 hours.

## 3. Holding limits

> No animal should stay in a store more than **60 days**.

| Days in store | Action |
|---|---|
| 45 | Manager reviews the animal's placement and display |
| 60 | Mark down up to 20% (see Coupons and Price Adjustments) or request a transfer |
| 90 | Animal Welfare arranges a transfer or a rescue partner |

The store system shows each animal's days in store under **Animals**.

## 4. When an animal leaves without a sale

Record it in the store system (**Animals → Remove Animal**) with the reason:

- **Deceased:** report to Animal Welfare within **24 hours** and file an incident report.
- **Returned to breeder:** attach the breeder's acknowledgment.
- **Transferred out of the chain:** to a rescue partner arranged by Animal Welfare.
