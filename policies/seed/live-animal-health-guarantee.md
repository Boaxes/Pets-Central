## 1. The guarantee

Every live animal sold by Pets Central (birds, small animals, reptiles and fish) is guaranteed healthy for **14 days**
from the day of sale.

## 2. What the customer needs

1. The receipt, or the sale found in the store system.
2. For birds, small animals and reptiles: a note from a licensed veterinarian dated within the 14 days, if the animal
   is ill. No note is needed if the animal is returned in its first **3 days** for any reason.
3. Fish: a water sample from the customer's tank, which the store tests before approving.

## 3. What the customer gets

- A refund of what they paid for the animal, or an exchange for an animal of the same species.
- Up to **$100** of documented vet costs, paid by corporate on submission of the invoice.

## 4. What happens to a returned animal

> A returned animal is **never put back on sale**. It goes to quarantine in the back room and is reported to Animal
> Welfare the same day.

Record the return in the store system with the reason. Do not mark it for restock: the store system does not restock
returned animals.

## 5. Outside the guarantee

After 14 days, a store manager may still accept an animal a customer can no longer keep, for no refund, so it is
cared for properly. Treat it as a returned animal (section 4).
